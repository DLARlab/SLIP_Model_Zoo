# Prospective local connection from imported PK to the low-energy PIP daughter

The independent low-energy audit found a nearby imported PK point and an accepted daughter at parent-kernel amplitude −0.01. Their saved apex distance is 0.00037341965 and energy difference is 1.26529948×10⁻⁶. These are different sampled states and energies. Proximity alone does not establish identity or attachment. This experiment asks whether four small, independently corrected increments on the same constrained physical family reach the frozen daughter.

The immutable registration is [low_energy_PK_bridge_registration.json](../Research_v3/next_round/low_energy_PK_bridge_registration.json). Its candidate retains the original imported `family_-1` point 2, the exact source snapshots and SHA-256 hashes, the independently accepted −0.01 daughter, the saved low-energy parent direction, current model and solver hashes, and the executed solver/warm-Jacobian gates. The preparation executes no return maps. The earlier independent audit is [Low_Energy_PIP_Critical_Independent_Audit_v3.md](Low_Energy_PIP_Critical_Independent_Audit_v3.md).

The physical model is the ten-parameter baseline `[10,10,20,20,1,1,0,0,2,0.5]`, with exact synchronized-pronk restriction, flight apex, and one actual BL touchdown per return. The original source has pitch/rate and duplicate-leg differences at roundoff scale; the candidate explicitly records canonicalization to zero pitch/rate and common leg states, along with its measured displacement. The original state is retained. No horizontal reflection is applied to this chosen orientation.

The observable is the saved parent direction dotted with `(dx, common leg angle, common leg rate)`. The direction is used without re-normalization. Its five-dimensional constrained energy chart has state coordinates `(dx,y,α,β)` and free orbit energy `E`; its normal has zero height and energy components. The five target amplitudes are the source observable −0.01044517977286106 followed by four equally spaced increments ending at −0.01. The source first undergoes fresh shared-service admission at its own observable. That evidence is counted separately from continuation progress.

The first new predictor follows the parent direction and sets `y=E−dx²/2`. Subsequent predictors use the preceding two accepted points' secant, with an explicit projection onto the next registered observable. Every solve retains its predictor, actual map traces, Newton report, full closure, and tighter independent replay. A finite reliable 5×5 final Jacobian is reused only within the same fixed model/chart/constraint normal and current source epoch. Partial best candidates and reliable Jacobians are preserved at wall expiry. The combined numerical budget is 480 seconds across resumes; four increments and locality bounds are fixed prospectively. Fresh admission is limited to a state displacement of 10⁻⁶; each continuation increment is limited to a state change of 5×10⁻⁴ and energy change of 10⁻⁵. All usual full closure, energy, stance, and primitive gates remain unchanged at 10⁻⁸.

The endpoint comparison uses the same labeled BL-marked apex, the same horizontal orientation, and a constant horizontal-origin shift. It records apex-state, energy, period, and drift differences; every actual TD/LO identity and count; both pre-reset and post-reset states; normalized contact phases; and right-continuous saved histories sampled only within smooth spans. Same-time virtual intermediate reset rows are excluded by the one-sided data contract. The saved interpolation is a finite-resolution diagnostic, not a new closure check or a certified trajectory bound. Its thresholds are frozen in the registration: apex/event state and drift 10⁻⁶, energy/period 10⁻⁷, event phase 10⁻⁶, smooth-history gap 10⁻⁴. Raw trajectories and comparison arrays are saved.

Four accepted local increments plus a compatible endpoint comparison would support a **local numerical continuation connection and endpoint orbit identity** within this exact restriction. A corrector that jumps directly to the target would not provide that evidence. Failure or budget exhaustion remains unresolved and is not evidence of nonexistence. This experiment does not establish global ancestry, unrestricted smoothness, a rigorous bifurcation certificate, or full stability.

Before activation, source inspection found that `PoincareMap_v3` stores `stride_displacement=rawReturnState−sectionState`, a 14-component state difference. An unindexed logical mean-speed gate would therefore fail after a valid service return. [Method revision 001](../Research_v3/next_round/low_energy_PK_bridge_method_revision_001.json) preserves the original candidate and registration hashes, replaces only the driver hash, and uses component `(1)` for scalar horizontal speed and drift. It also explicitly checks the recorded maximum pitch and exactly eight actual contact events against the registered pitch/event bounds. MATLAB read-only inspection confirmed 14×1 displacement vectors in both frozen source and target, pitch maxima 4.2448×10⁻¹⁶ and zero, and eight contact events each. The revised no-map public-driver/hash/equation/self-comparison preflight passed in 0.476 seconds; no new simulations or return maps were performed. The physical model, amplitude grid, 480-second budget, and full acceptance thresholds did not change.
