# Primitive period two in the opposed-side-spread PIP restriction

This is a separate analytic route in the ideal model at the exact parameters `p=[10,10,20,20,1,1,0,0,2,0.5]`. It changes neither the traveling-pronk theorem nor the registered unrestricted front/hind pilots. The new restriction has `dx=phi=dphi=0`, equal contact bits, and in v3 leg order `[BL,BR,FL,FR]` the angles/rates are `[a,−a,a,−a]` and `[b,−b,b,−b]`. Actual gait naming is downstream of a full orbit; this restriction is not automatically B2, F2 or G2.

## Invariance and an analytic physical return

All four legs have the same length/compression because `cos(−a)=cos(a)` and pitch is zero. Left/right horizontal forces cancel; front/back torques cancel for each side because the matched offsets are `−0.5,+0.5`. In all-leg flight the body follows `y''=−1`, and each side angle obeys `a''=−20a`. In all-leg stance the common projected rate is

`a'=−(y'/y)*sin(a)*cos(a)`.

It is odd in a. The constrained angular acceleration is its time derivative, so the paired-opposite rates remain paired-opposite. Every guard is the common function `g=y−cos(a)` and has the same Lie derivative. The full common TD or LO reset replaces b by the displayed projected rate and preserves the restriction. Partial scalar reset steps commute and their complete physical post-state returns to this restriction. [Flow and constraint equations](../1_Dynamic_Frameworks/Dynamics_v3/ContinuousDynamics_v3.m), [actual common guards](../1_Dynamic_Frameworks/Dynamics_v3/GuardFunctions_v3.m), [rate projection and batch reset](../1_Dynamic_Frameworks/Dynamics_v3/ResetMap_v3.m).

On the all-flight apex the exact energy is y, because horizontal/pitch velocities vanish and the model energy contains no massless swing kinetic or torsional term. Thus the fixed-energy section coordinates are `u=(a,b)`, `y=E`. During stance, `c=y*tan(a)` is constant and the four-leg spring energy is `20*(1−sqrt(y²+c²))²`. [Model-owned energy](../1_Dynamic_Frameworks/Dynamics_v3/QuadrupedEnergy_v3.m).

The vertical parent at `u=0`, `1<E<20`, has flight half-duration `t=sqrt(2(E−1))`, positive stance height, strictly positive interior compression, TD/LO speeds `−t,+t`, and a transverse flight apex. At each fixed parent these margins persist for small u. The flow/reset formulae have analytic extensions for positive y and `cos(a)>0`; the common event times are analytic by the implicit function theorem. All four contacts remain one common cohort in this exact restriction. The resulting single-BL-marked return `P_E(u)` is analytic near the parent. This does not establish C² regularity of unrestricted contact-order charts.

The simultaneous side-leg swap `(BL BR)(FL FR)` sends u to −u while preserving the fixed parameters. It preserves the common guard times and the accepted marked apex: BL and BR TD coincide, so changing the labeled mark does not change the section owner. Hence `P_E(−u)=−P_E(u)`. No arbitrary leg permutation or a changed-model symmetry is used. [BL occurrence ownership](../4_Solution_Management/BLMarkedApexReturnPolicy_v3.m).

## Simple twisted crossing

Put `w=sqrt(20)` and `F(t)=[[cos(wt),sin(wt)/w],[-w*sin(wt),cos(wt)]]`. First-order vertical and event-time changes vanish at u=0. TD drops the incoming rate coordinate; linear stance transport obeys `a'=−y'a/y`, giving the same angle at its y=1 endpoints; LO has `b=−t*a`. Consequently

`M(E)=D_u P_E(0)=F(t)*[1;−t]*[1,0]*F(t)`.

Its eigenvalues are zero and

`lambda(E)=cos(2*w*t)−(t/w)*sin(2*w*t)`.

At `E_n=1+(2n+1)²*pi²/160`, `n=0,...,8`, `lambda=−1` and `lambda_E=2`. In particular `M(E_n)+I=[[1,−t/20],[0,0]]`, with one-dimensional kernel generated by `[t/20;1]`, one-dimensional cokernel generated by `[0;1]`, and nonzero parameter crossing. For `G(u,E)=P_E(u)+u`, analyticity and these conditions give a local nonzero curve of twisted fixed points by the simple-eigenvalue bifurcation theorem. [Original Crandall–Rabinowitz theorem 1.7](https://jxshix.people.wm.edu/2013-taiwan/crandall-rabinowitz-1971-JFA.pdf).

Oddness makes each twisted fixed point satisfy `P_E²(u)=u`. It is not a one-return fixed point, since `P_E(u)=−u` and u is nonzero. Near the parent each one-return leg itinerary has one common TD and LO and one flight. A labeled smaller period must repeat a TD and therefore would give the one-return apex state; this is excluded by `P_E(u)≠u`. Thus the resulting physical labeled cycle has primitive BL occurrence 2, two TD/LO events per leg and two positive flight intervals. A side-leg permutation after one return is a spatiotemporal symmetry, not a labeled physical period reduction.

## An explicit finite-amplitude construction

There is also a direct construction, independent of estimating nonlinear coefficients numerically. Let

`t_n=(2n+1)*pi/(2*w)`, `epsilon=(−1)^n`,

`E(A)=cos(A)+t_n²/2`,

`u(A)=[epsilon*(t_n/w)*sin(A); epsilon*w*A]`.

For sufficiently small signed TD angle A, descending flight lasts exactly `t_n`, reaches `y=cos(A)`, angle A and vertical velocity `−t_n`. The incoming swing rate is `−t_n*sin(A)`; TD projects it to `+t_n*sin(A)`. In stance `c=sin(A)` is fixed. At LO the zero-compression condition again gives `y=cos(A)` and angle A, while energy conservation gives vertical velocity `+t_n` and projected rate `−t_n*sin(A)`. The next flight-apex time is `t_n`. Applying the quarter-turn F gives exactly `P_E(u(A))=−u(A)`.

An explicit sufficient interval is `0<|A|<0.01` for every listed resonance `n=0,...,8`. This interval is conservative; it is not a numerically fitted maximal domain. To see that these are first physical roots rather than algebraic endpoint matches, write

`V_c(y)=y+20*(1−sqrt(y²+c²))²`, `c=sin(A)`, `y_0=cos(A)`.

The energy at contact is `E=V_c(y_0)+t_n²/2`. The barrier satisfies `V_c(0)>20*(0.99)²=19.602`, while `E≤E_8<19`. At `y=y_0`, `V_c'=1`; at `y=0.5`, `V_c'<0`; and for nonzero c, `V_c''=40*(1−c²/(y²+c²)^(3/2))` is strictly increasing on positive y. Thus `V_c'` has exactly two zeros: a small-height maximum and a higher-height minimum. The maximum exceeds `V_c(0)>E`, and the minimum is below `V_c(y_0)<E`. Descending from `y_0` therefore reaches a unique first energy turning point between these two extrema, with positive y and `V_c'<0`. The turn is regular and takes finite time. The upward path reaches `y_0` again with `y'=t_n`. For all interior stance times `0<y<y_0`, the common length is less than one, so compression is strictly positive. The only positive-height zero-compression root along this excursion is the final `y=y_0`. TD post-reset and LO pre/post-reset guard derivatives are respectively `−t_n*cos²(A)` and `+t_n*cos²(A)`.

In either flight segment, the exact harmonic angle/rate bounds give `|sin(a)*a'|≤C_n*A²`, where `C_n=w*(1+t_n/w)²`. Descending-flight clearance is at least `(t_n−s)*(t_n/2−C_n*A²)` for `0≤s<t_n`; upward-flight clearance is at least `s*(t_n/2−C_n*A²)` for `0<s≤t_n`. These follow by integrating the cosine difference from its zero endpoint. On the stated interval, `C_n*A²<0.0026<t_0/2`, so both clearances are strictly positive. There is no earlier TD during descending flight, and the upward flight stays airborne until its transverse apex. The stance turn has upward apex orientation and does not qualify as the downward-apex section. Thus the marked BL occurrence and the claimed primitive two-return itinerary are preserved throughout this interval. The A=0 parent is the limiting period-one orbit.

This constructs nonzero labeled two-cycles directly. They have zero horizontal drift. Within this new exact restriction the formula proves `E'(0)=0`, `E''(0)=−1`; this is restricted branch geometry, not an unrestricted pitchfork or flip normal form. The earlier traveling-pronk proof still makes no quadratic-coefficient claim.

## Opposite signs are two phases of the same cycle

At fixed n and nonzero magnitude |A|, `E(A)=E(−A)` and `u(−A)=−u(A)`. The exact single-marked return therefore satisfies `P_BL1(x_n(A))=x_n(−A)` and then `P_BL1(x_n(−A))=x_n(A)`. Both signed apex states lie on the same autonomous labeled physical orbit. The common stance potential depends on `sin²(A)` and its endpoint height on `cos(A)`, so the one-return duration is the same for both signs; the time shift is exactly half the full BL2 period. This equivalence keeps the leg labels and applies no leg permutation. It does not reduce the primitive period: the two apex states differ when A is nonzero, so a half-period shift does not return the full labeled state to itself.

Thus ±A are two marked apex phases of one primitive two-return cycle modulo time phase, not two distinct orbit classes or gait families. The four frozen n0/n1 signed predictors at |A|=0.001 give two sampled cycle classes modulo phase, one at each distinct resonance energy. This is not a count of globally distinct gait families or a statement about their global connection. Replay, predictor admission and nonlinear correction remain separate numerical evidence operations even when they describe the same physical cycle.
