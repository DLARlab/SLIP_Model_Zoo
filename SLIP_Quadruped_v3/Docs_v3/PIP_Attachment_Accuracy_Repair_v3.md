# Targeted signed-daughter attachment accuracy repair

The executed revision02 repairs for `PIP_candidate_1` and `PIP_PK_low_energy_neighborhood` both pass physical and registered-domain acceptance and every unchanged restricted-attachment gate. Each successful repair replaces only the positive +0.0003 point in an independent copy of the ten-record sequence. The original sequences, daughter files, task counters and their `candidate_only` outcomes remain unchanged. This establishes conditional numerical local attachment within the synchronized fixed-parameter pronk subspace; it makes no full-space C2, stability or global-network claim.

The original ten-daughter sequences each pass physical periodicity and fresh tighter replay, but fail the separate quadratic-energy and signed-reflection attachment gates. Saved-MAT inspection isolates both discrepancies to the positive +0.0003 point. Every other equal-magnitude signed pair passes the registered `1e-7` reflection threshold.

Both reports identify the solver as `residual-check`, with “Initial point satisfies the root tolerance.” The shared service's effective initial shortcut threshold was `ResidualAcceptanceTolerance=1e-10`. This is the configured old residual threshold; it is insufficient to resolve nearcritical energy accurately enough for these additional attachment gates. The daughter caller also omitted the configured `FunctionTolerance=1e-12`, so the service used its default `1e-11`, but changing only that function tolerance would still allow the initial shortcut. This diagnosis does not invalidate the recorded physical periodicity acceptance at the unchanged `1e-8` full-state closure gate.

The corresponding reduced-coordinate reflection discrepancies are only `4.18e-12` and `2.91e-11`; energy differences are `3.83557e-8` and `4.65401e-8`. Period supplies the maximum reflection discrepancy. Internal physical versus augmented energy consistency is exactly zero in both saved reports, so the anomaly is not an energy-evaluation inconsistency. The incorrect negative energy shifts exceed the fit's resolution cutoff `5*5e-9`, adding one spurious resolved small-amplitude point to each positive fit. Remaining positive resolved points have relative fit errors at most `0.01137` and `0.03096`, below the existing `0.15` threshold.

The saved unused warm matrices have condition estimates `2.23e4` and `4.10e5`; their algebraic remaining-energy steps are `+1.054e-7` and `+1.867e-6`. These matrices came from a different amplitude and are approximate warm starts, not newly validated current derivatives or forward-error bounds. The actual saved negative -0.0003 final linear models have condition estimates `1.05e4` and `1.51e4`, and remaining-energy estimates `+1.124e-8` and `+2.238e-8`. This supports weak nearcritical energy determination despite small state residuals; it does not establish a singular corrector or an unrestricted regularity theorem. The algebra-only audits made zero return-map calls and are preserved in `P1_Single_Flight_Phase_Continua/Bifurcation_Audits/PIP_to_PK/Stopping_Audit/` and `pip_attachment_stopping_low/`.
