Why the requested PK branch was not finished

1. The selected component was not qualified against the P1 period family.

The final native comparison exposes a material selection error. The extended
0072 component has periods T=[2.613199361582759,2.822003761557232], whereas the
original P1 stored periods are [0.8967107863773467,1.564520214496882]. Energy
and velocity plots can look close while the return periods differ. A common
PK classification therefore did not establish that continuation was following
the desired P1 branch. Period/template compatibility should have been checked
before choosing the focused frontier. The new solutions are retained as valid
partial PK data, but are not counted as proof of completing the P1 family.
No connection between 0072 and the shorter-period 0073 component was proved.
Disjoint observed period ranges alone are not a global proof that the components
could never connect elsewhere.

The shorter-period component 0073 is the appropriate current candidate for
further P1-template qualification: its T=[1.372602811539128,1.564516132250715]
overlaps the stored period range of P1 records passing the initial-geometry
screen. Its ranges are
E=[1.117158075715542,8.875604931997586] and signed initial
dx=[-0.03433997446604415,3.968779293742992]. It is still a finite fragment;
its true endpoints, full P1 identity and any seams to 0159/0193 are unverified.

2. Most original P1 records fail the current initial physical geometry check.

P1 contains 891 PK records with E=[1.1171389428139562,208.89093745793602]
and signed initial dx=[-0.0073364571486461009,20.423184056395353]. Only 179
pass the recorded native initial-geometry check; 712 fail it. The passing
subset spans E=[1.117138942813956,8.817664370005977] and
dx=[-0.007336457148646101,3.954042303119467]. Passing initial geometry is
not a claim of native trajectory admission or a complete branch.

The exact original maximum-dx record, column 600, was replayed without repairing
its full initial state or parameters, using the reference audit's documented
right-limit scheduled mode. Native simulation rejected it at t=0, before any
ODE segment: all four swing feet violate nonpenetration/contact complementarity.
The full raw native result is retained. The original exporter failed after
saving that result; a separate native custody finalizer installed its exact
bytes. Original failed-producer flags were preserved, trace return timeliness
remains explicitly unknown, and this invalid source was never admitted.

Source checks found matching geometry, leg order/signs, body-relative angles,
parameter conversion and stance booleans. Both versions use
h_i=y+s_i*sin(phi)-l_i*cos(phi+alpha_i). Legacy zero-time events may reset leg
rates before positive-time integration; those resets do not change foot heights.
This is source-level agreement, not complete hybrid-equivalence proof. We did
not freshly evaluate the legacy scheduled shooting residual for all 891 records.

The v2 residual uses supplied contact times and scheduled endpoint conditions.
The v3 map additionally requires autonomous first directed events and physical
inequalities along the accepted path. A stored v2 root is not automatically
admissible in v3. Requiring the entire old range without resolving these failed
records is an unresolved research requirement; it cannot be silently replaced
by a claim that the 179-record initial-geometry subset is the full target.

Two native necessary-bound audits support this caution for the exact upright,
equal-leg, regular one-stance/one-flight forward pronk chart, under their explicit
reset, raw-state closure and positive-height/orientation assumptions. With
p=[10,10,20,20,1,1,0,0,2,0.5] and nondimensional m=g=l=1, the speed derivation
excludes the P1 maximum velocity; its reported critical necessary bound is
approximately 15.115159151695822. Combining that theorem with a conservative
height bound yields E<202, excluding the P1 maximum energy in that exact class.
These are conditional bounds, not attained maxima, verified numerical endpoints,
or global nonexistence proofs for all tolerance-classified PK trajectories.

3. Numerical obstructions and restart defects were not endpoint proofs.

Near E about 31.12747 on the longer-period PK fragment, registered root
strategies encounter inadmissible initial maps, invalid derivative columns or
inadmissible Newton trial directions. Small periodicity residual alone is
insufficient: the PAL selector, physical replay, primitive identity and every
other admission gate must pass. Exhausting the three registered strategies
is a recorded numerical obstruction, not a certified endpoint or bifurcation.

This evidence explains concrete obstacles in the retained runs, not every hour

Loading, resuming and files

Add the v3 root with V3AddPaths_v3 and add Research_v3/C1. Use
BranchSolutionFile_v3.loadSolution(file,index), or loadSolutions(file,indices),
to retrieve individual native orbits/reports/metadata. The GUI opens these MAT
files directly. Creation catalogues are immutable snapshots; current grown-file
hashes belong to the central frontier ledger and final live inventory.

The historical published file
P1_Single_Flight_Phase_Continua/C1_PK_10_20_2.mat already exists. It was not
replaced by a falsely completed branch; its contents remain historical.

ResumeC1PKFrontiers_v3 preserves component order, actual secants, arclength steps,
derivative strategies, failed attempts and current file ownership. It appends
to the component MAT, rather than producing one file per point. MaxBatches and
MaxWallSeconds are per-call caps; PreferredFrontierID selects an exact runnable
registered frontier. The default scheduler explores registered PK graphs;
registration by gait label does NOT prove P1-family identity. A later authorized
grant requires explicit native capture and AdoptNewGrant=true after old children
are reconciled. Resume alone never renews the captured clock. Keep the permanent
zero-byte .writer.lock file: it is part of the exclusive-writer mechanism.

Remaining decision and work

The full P1/v2 PK range remains unresolved. Retaining v3's physical admission
rules requires treating the 712 failed P1 records as unqualified reference data,
then explicitly deciding the valid comparison target and qualifying the shorter-
period 0073 family against it. Changing physical/event semantics to reproduce
the entire old range would be a different research decision. Neither change was
made silently. Numerical continuation and verified seams/endpoints/bifurcations
are still needed after that target/identity issue is settled.

No complete PK, BG, BE, FG, FE, HG, HE, GG or GE C1 branch is claimed. The full
PK-to-GG/GE bifurcation chain, including the expected FG/HE attachments, remains
unverified. C2/PIP-to-B2 was excluded. H means hind spread/fore sync; F means
fore spread/hind sync.
