4. Why full PK completion remains unresolved

The target is not established to be one native family. P1 has 891 stored PK
columns, energy 1.1171389428139562 .. 208.89093745793602, signed INITIAL dx
-0.0073364571486461 .. 20.423184056395353, and stored period
0.8967107863773467 .. 1.564520214496882. Native mean stride velocity is a separate
observable; comparing P1 initial dx20.423 directly with native mean U7.70 would
be invalid. The current native initial-dx maximum is about 7.72945.

Only 179 P1 columns pass the native initial-geometry check; 712 fail. The passing
subset reaches initial dx3.95404230312 and E8.81766437001. This is an INITIAL
geometry census, not a trajectory/root-admission census. Legacy timed event
schedules and the v3 autonomous directed first-event map have different domain
requirements. No parameter/order/sign conversion defect has been established
that licenses relabeling all legacy columns as current native solutions.

For the exact upright, synchronous, principal-angle, regular, forward-monotone
one-flight/one-stance pronk class, the necessary speed threshold is estimated
at about15.115159; this floating estimate is not an interval certificate.
The retained separate exact rational contradiction excludes V>=20 in the stated
class, and therefore excludes P1's maximum initial speed. Its separate
forward-monotone-class energy bound is E<202. These are conditional necessary
bounds, not attained maxima, a global no-PK theorem or a membership proof for
every tolerance-qualified stored PK point. The user selected continued
admissible native-fragment tracing under this limitation.

Long-period native fragments (for example0072,T2.613199..2.849185) are outside
P1's period range. Short-period0073 overlaps it, and0193 approaches the same
unit-height neighborhood, but period/energy/speed overlap does not prove native
family identity, graph connection, full reference coverage or bifurcation.
Negative-velocity extensions also do not fill missing positive P1 coverage.

Actual local refusals include invalid initial return, tensile stance, failed
full13 closure/replay and unavailable admissible finite-difference Jacobians.

an admitted physical periodic orbit. The ordinary all-flight PK root service

Newton solve. Independent full13 physical closure remains required. No single
cause is asserted for every failed trial, and finite one-sided samples do not
certify a smooth full neighborhood near a changing event domain.
