Exact synchronous upright one-flight class: exclusion of a grounded BL-marked apex
Date: 2026-11-04. Status: analytic argument checked against the source below.
This audit contains no MATLAB execution, numerical experiment or global search claim.

Claim and assumptions

Consider an exact relative-periodic solution of the unmodified native v3 model,
with mass and gravity normalized to one, in the following restricted class:

1. Matched back/front axial stiffness k>0 and rest length L>0, centered COM
   l_com=1/2, upright torso phi=dphi=0, and all four raw leg angles equal to a
   common continuous alpha. The intended parameters are
   p=[10;10;20;20;1;1;0;0;2;.5]. The argument uses the actual restricted orbit;
   a label or approximate equality of saved coordinates is insufficient.
2. One positive-duration, connected all-stance interval S and one positive-
   duration, connected all-flight interval F per period, separated by one
   simultaneous four-leg LO cohort and one simultaneous four-leg TD cohort.
   There are no partial-contact modes, extra contact loops, zero-duration
   phases, chatter, Zeno accumulation or unresolved grazing/coincident events.
3. Exact continuous physical admissibility: y>0, cos(alpha)>0 throughout,
   0<r=y/cos(alpha)<=L in stance, and swing clearance
   g=y-L*cos(alpha)>=0 throughout flight. Stance compression is positive on
   some open interval. At the regular LO/TD endpoints, r=L and g=0 exactly.
4. Stance feet are fixed horizontally. Native resets leave torso position,
   torso velocity and raw angles continuous. The periodic state closes
   exactly except for horizontal translation. No angle wrapping, projection,
   force clamp or modified reset is introduced.
5. The section uses the native BL-last-downward-apex-before-next-TD marking,
   with strict time separation and transverse downward COM apexes.

Then F contains a strict interior downward COM apex. Consequently the last
downward COM apex strictly before its terminating BL TD is in all-flight.
A grounded COM apex cannot be the BL-marked initial or return representative
of an orbit satisfying these assumptions. This does not exclude other,
unmarked grounded apexes during S.

Proof

Choose one stance interval [0,T_S] from TD to LO. For any leg let
xi=hip_x-foot_x=-y*tan(alpha)=-r*sin(alpha). All four xi coincide because the
upright hip offsets are constant. Fixed feet give xi'=body_dx=v. Native force
summation gives

  xi''=v'=-4*k*(L-r)*sin(alpha)=a(t)*xi,
  a(t)=4*k*(L-r)/r >= 0.

On a regular stance interval a is continuous and finite, and a>0 on an open
subinterval. Flight has v'=0, and resets preserve v. Exact velocity
periodicity therefore gives v(0)=v(T_S)=V, the same constant horizontal
velocity as on F.

Suppose V>0. If v first reaches zero at t*, then xi(t*) cannot be positive:
xi would be positive on a preceding neighborhood, where v'=a*xi>=0, so v
could not decrease from positive values to zero. If xi(t*)=0, uniqueness for
xi''=a(t)*xi with xi=v=0 contradicts v(0)=V. Thus xi(t*)<0. The closed cone
{xi<=0,v<=0} is forward invariant under xi'=v, v'=a*xi with a>=0. It could
not subsequently reach v(T_S)=V>0. Hence v>0 throughout S and xi increases
strictly. Moreover

  0=v(T_S)-v(0)=integral_0^T_S a(t)*xi(t) dt.

The positive-compression interval and strict increase force
xi_TD<0<xi_LO: either nonnegative xi_TD or nonpositive xi_LO would make the
integral have a strict nonzero sign. At contact r=L, so
sin(alpha_TD)>0 and sin(alpha_LO)<0.

Now follow F from this LO to the next TD; exact periodicity repeats the TD
contact coordinates just used. Raw alpha is continuous, and
cos(alpha)>0 keeps it inside one connected interval
(-pi/2+2*pi*n, pi/2+2*pi*n). The LO angle is below 2*pi*n and the TD angle
is above it. Therefore alpha=2*pi*n at an interior flight time t0. This is
an intermediate-value argument; no monotonicity of alpha or assumed swing
frequency is needed. Winding by 2*pi would require passing cos(alpha)<=0
and is excluded by continuous downward orientation.

Flight nonpenetration at t0 gives y(t0)>=L. At both contact endpoints the
strict nonzero sine gives y=L*cos(alpha)<L. Since native all-flight motion
has y''=-1, its height is a strictly concave parabola with an interior value
larger than both endpoints. Its unique vertex is strictly inside F, with
y'=0 and y''=-1<0. This is the required transverse flight COM apex.

For V<0 apply the same argument to (-xi,-v). The endpoint sine signs and
angle traversal reverse, but again alpha crosses 2*pi*n inside F and the
same ballistic conclusion follows.

For V=0, multiply xi''=a*xi by xi and integrate over S:

  -integral_0^T_S (xi')^2 dt = integral_0^T_S a*xi^2 dt >= 0,

because [xi*xi']_0^T_S=0. Thus xi'=0 throughout S; positive compression
somewhere then forces xi=0. At TD and LO, downward orientation gives
alpha=2*pi*n and y=L. A positive-duration ballistic flight returning from
y=L to y=L has y'_LO=T_F/2>0, y'_TD=-T_F/2<0 and a strict interior apex
of height L+T_F^2/8. This establishes the apex result without a gait label
or a separate PIP-classification theorem.

In all cases F ends at the next BL TD and contains no other contact event.
Its unique downward apex is later than every preceding stance apex and
strictly before that TD. The native BL marking therefore selects this
flight apex and rejects a grounded apex before it. QED.

Practical application limits

- This closes the grounded-BL-apex alternative only inside the exact class
  and hypotheses above. Stop new grounded-apex acquisition in that class;
  preserve completed failed trials as methodological evidence, not an
  absence proof or an admitted branch. No dynamics or admission rule changes.
- A saved all-nine result with 1e-8 closure, stance-tension and penetration
  allowances is not automatically an exact solution covered by the theorem.
  Sampled orientation/clearance checks do not establish continuous bounds.
  A rigorous numerical application needs a nearby exact-orbit justification
  and interval/error bounds, or a quantified robust inequality argument with
  sufficient strict margins. Those are not supplied by this text audit.
- Do not apply the conclusion to a tilted or asymmetric orbit, nonmatched
  parameters, partial-contact timing, multiple S/F intervals, winding allowed
  by disabling downward orientation, tension, penetration, singular contact,
  zero-duration/chattering phases, or an altered reset/section policy.
- The result neither proves that all PK or C1 solutions have these symmetries
  nor proves uniqueness, connectedness, finite-frontier exhaustiveness, full
  P1/v2 correspondence, PK absence outside the class, or the complete branch
  search. It supplies no PIP classification or global gait-family theorem.
- Continuous-flight validation and the fresh 0073/0193 seam experiment remain
  separate numerical obligations. Historical qualified M stays historical;
  this argument creates no P edge, M relation or transferred admission flag.
